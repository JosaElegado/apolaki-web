## Section 3

Heading stays **"Who it's for."** The lede now sets up why three groups matter at all:

> Nobody goes solar alone. Homeowners, installers and lenders all need the same thing: numbers they can trust. Here's what each one gets.

Cards went from roughly 18 words back up to around 35. Enough to actually explain the value, still short of a paragraph.

**Homeowners, "Know before you spend"**
What size your roof needs, what it costs all in, and how much you'll save each month. Then a short list of installers we've already checked, so you're not guessing who to call.

**Installers, "Meet people who are ready"**
Everyone who reaches you already knows their system size, their budget and what they'll save. No cold lists, no chasing. And you're ranked on the jobs you've finished, not on what you spend on ads.

**Financing partners, "Lend on real numbers"**
Every borrower comes with a real electricity bill behind them, so you can see what they use and what they can afford. Same specs on every project, and you can check how the panels perform after.

The last clause of each card does the persuading: *so you're not guessing who to call*, *not on what you spend on ads*, *check how the panels perform after*. That is the value, in words each group would use themselves.

## Design system

**Type:** Figtree, one family for display and body, subset and inlined at 11KB.

**Colour:** dark sections built from your brand navy `#0A2247`, surfaces `#143460`, ink and hairlines blue-tinted. Brand blue `#0E6CBD` for actions, gold `#F2C94C` used sparingly on the H1 emphasis, section tags and link arrows.

**Motion:** one easing curve throughout, hero parallax, counting numbers, staggered reveals, and the scroll-driven phone sequence. All disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
